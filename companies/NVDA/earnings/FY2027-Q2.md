## 5. 管理层电话会核心要点（Jensen Huang & Colette Kress）

### 5.1 历史性指引突破：破例给出 FY2028 全年增速 ~70%
- **CFO Colette Kress 官方定调**：
  > *"Looking ahead, our preliminary expectation is for fiscal year 2028 revenue to grow approximately 70% year-over-year. Although we will work to close the supply-demand gap, we expect supply to remain a bottleneck at least through the end of fiscal year 2028."*
  > *"Customers' forecasts point to our growth doubling next year. However, as I mentioned earlier, we expect to grow approximately 70% as we are supply-constrained."*
- **摩根士丹利（Morgan Stanley）Joe Moore 现场提问**：
  > *“I wonder if you could give us color on the 70%, and what gives you the confidence to guide a full year out? You haven't been doing that. And what's the gap between that amount and the 100% demand growth?”*
- **黄仁勋正面回应（破例打破历史惯例）**：
  > *“It is the case that we've never forecasted or never guided to a year in advance. And even though our demand is much greater than 70%, our supply allows us to confidently deliver 70%... At this moment, we have supply for 70%, we have more supply than 70%, but about 70%. Our demand is much higher than that.”*
- **Bernstein 分析师 Stacy Rasgon 测算**：
  > 该 70% 增速意味着英伟达在未来 3 年的指引基础上，直接产生了近 **$2,000 亿美元** 的超预期增量空间！

### 5.2 核心产品与需求态势
- **Blackwell 与 Vera Rubin 交付**：GB200 NVL72 机柜在各大顶级云厂商（微软、谷歌、Meta、AWS、CoreWeave）实现数万台规模化交付；Vera Rubin 预计将在 Q3 占到数据中心营收的 20%，并在 Q4 与 FY28 持续爬坡。
- **推理算力爆发（Test-time Compute）**：黄仁勋强调，随着具身智能与长链条思考模型的普及，单次 Prompt 推理调用的 Token 数呈指数级增加，推理业务贡献已超过数据中心总体计算算力的 **50%**。

### 5.3 核心原话（Quotes）
> **Jensen Huang**: *"Reasoning AI models and Agentic workflows are driving an unprecedented explosion in inference compute demand. We are witnessing the shift from static software to continuous, real-time token generation at global scale."*
